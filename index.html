Germ Hide & Seek is a memory card game about how our cells use MHC molecules to show the immune system which cells are infected — making hidden germs much easier to find.

Play full-screen

Add Germ Hide & Seek to your Home Screen to play it like an app: full-screen, without the browser bars, one tap away.

  1. Open this page in Safari. (Steps may differ slightly in other browsers.)
  2. Tap the Share button . On iPhone you may need to tap ••• first.
  3. Tap View More if needed, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, keep it switched on. Then tap Add.
  5. Open the game from the new icon on your Home Screen.
  1. Open this page in Chrome. (Steps may differ slightly in other browsers.)
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install.
  4. Open the game from the new icon on your Home Screen.
